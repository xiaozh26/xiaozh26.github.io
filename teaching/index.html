      The floating tool icon can be dragged anywhere; click it to open or close the toolbar. On iPad the lock icon (“Pencil only”, on by default) makes the slide ignore fingers and your resting palm entirely — only the Apple Pencil draws. Pick the hand tool (V) to pan and pinch-zoom with fingers; zoom and pan are mirrored on the laptop. Turn it off to pan and pinch-zoom with fingers; the hand icon then lets a finger draw.
